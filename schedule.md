# Overview schedule

The workshop will consist of two full days and a third half day at the Wrigley Institute on Catalina Island. Each day will include a mix of instruction, practical tutorials and followed time to work on exercises and personal projects. Lectures will be recorded for aynchronous viewing on YouTube. Participants are expected to give a short presentation on the final day of the workshop. The presentations will not be made publicly available. 

## All times are in PST (Los Angeles time)

### Day 1: Introduction to PReSto

Travel (via boat) to Catalina Island in the morning, then jumping into PReSto.

### Day 2: PReSto's Custom Reconstruction Engine

How to use PReSto to modify existing reconstructions, or create your own.

### Day 3: Contributing to PReSto and sharing your results

Participants' presentation. Return to Long Beach in the afternoon.
